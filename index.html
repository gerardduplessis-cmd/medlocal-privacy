Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Overview

MedLocal is a personal medical information app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is stored, and how you remain in control of it.

2. Information We Collect

MedLocal collects and stores the personal and health information that you choose to enter, which may include: Full name, date of birth, blood type, and emergency contact details Medications, dosages, and prescription expiry dates Allergies and reaction details Past surgeries and procedures Blood pressure readings Free-text notes and device information (e.g. implants) This information is collected only when you actively enter it into the app. MedLocal does not collect any data automatically, and does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage tracking data of any kind.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

All information you enter is stored exclusively on your own device, using local on-device storage. MedLocal has no cloud server, no user accounts, and no backend database. Nothing you enter is transmitted to MedLocal, its developer, or any third party, at any time, under any circumstance.

4. Consent

By entering information into MedLocal, you consent to that information being stored locally on your device as described in this policy. You may withdraw this consent at any time by deleting the app or clearing its data through your device's app settings, which permanently removes all stored information. MedLocal is not directed at children. If you are under the age of 18, please only use this app with the involvement and consent of a parent or guardian.

5. Data Sharing

MedLocal does not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your information to any third party, because your information never leaves your device in the first place.

6. Your Control Over Your Data

You may, at any time and entirely through the app itself: Export your data as a JSON backup file, which you control and store as you choose Import a previous backup to restore your data Delete individual records, or clear all app data via your device's settings

7. Data Retention

Your information remains on your device for as long as you keep the app installed and the data entered. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all stored information permanently and immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.

9. Contact

f you have questions about this policy, please reach out via the repository or support channel listed on the app's store listing page.
