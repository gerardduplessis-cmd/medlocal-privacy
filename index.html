Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Overview

MedLocal is designed with privacy as its absolute core principle. We believe your medical history and health records belong strictly to you.

2. Zero Data Collection & Transmission

MedLocal does not collect, track, store, or transmit any personal, medical, or usage data to external servers, cloud storage, or third-party analytics platforms.

3. On-Device Storage Only

All patient details, medications, allergies, surgeries, blood pressure logs, and notes are saved exclusively and securely on your device using local browser storage and IndexedDB. Nothing ever leaves your device.

4. User Ownership & Control

You maintain total control over your data. You can back up your records into a local JSON file or wipe your data completely at any time through the app settings. No account creation or internet connection is required to use the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, you can reach out via our repository or support channels.
